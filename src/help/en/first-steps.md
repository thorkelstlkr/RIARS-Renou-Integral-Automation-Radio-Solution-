# First steps

This is the shortest path from an empty RIARS to a station that plays by itself.

## 1. Add music to the library

Open the desktop app and find the **Library** area on the left. Click **Add folder** and choose a folder of audio files. RIARS scans it and adds the tracks to the library database. A message tells you how many new tracks were added. You can add more folders later; the list grows as you scroll.

## 2. Build a playlist

Find a track in the library and **double-click** it. The track is added to the **Playlist** tab. Repeat for the first few songs. You can also search the library with the **Search...** box, and remove or reorder items in the playlist.

## 3. Play

Click **Play** in the transport controls at the top. The **On air** area shows the current track with its progress and time left; the right-hand **Now Playing** card shows the same information. **Pause**, **Stop** and **Next** work as you would expect.

## 4. Turn on automation

On the right, find the **Automation** card and click its toggle so it reads **ON**. In a new installation the generator has nothing to play yet: it needs at least a category, a clock and something in the grid.

A simple setup:

1. Open the **Categories** tab and click **New**. Give the category a name, choose the kind **Music**, save it, then add tracks to it.
2. Open the **Clocks** tab. Create a clock with **Add slot**, choose the **Fill** type and pick your music category. Save the clock.
3. Open the **Grid** tab and create a rule that plays that clock every day, all day. Save it.
4. In **Categories**, open **Station settings** and choose the clock as **Default clock**. Save the settings.

The generator now keeps a few items ahead of the air. It adds them to the end of the playlist and never cuts what is already playing.

Read [Automation overview](topic:automation-overview) for the whole picture, and [Library](topic:library) and [Playlist](topic:playlist) for the details.
