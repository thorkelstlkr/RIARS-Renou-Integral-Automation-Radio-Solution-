The main window

The desktop window is laid out like a broadcast console: what is on air first, then the tools, then the automation setup.

Top of the window

The header holds the RIARS logo, the transport controls (Play, Pause, Stop, Next and the Vol slider), the streaming badge and the language selector.

Below it, the On air bar shows, from left to right:

  • the date, the local time and the time left of the track on air;
  • Previous, the track that just finished;
  • On air, the current track, with a progress bar and its position and duration;
  • Next, the track that will follow.

Items that are not music show their kind next to the label, such as Jingle, Spot or Station ID.

Left, centre and right

The left sidebar contains the Library, with its Library and Folders tabs.

The centre holds eight tabs: Playlist, Schedule, Clocks, Grid, Categories, Advertising, Log and Live (see Live broadcasting). Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8 switch between them in that order.

The right sidebar contains the Now Playing card, the VU Meter, the Log pane with recent proof-of-play entries and automation warnings, and the Automation card with its ON/OFF toggle.

The bottom StatusBar shows the silence detector (Silence detector: on or off, or a red SILENCE ON AIR), the automation state (Automation: on or off), the number of active scheduled events and the RIARS version.

Menus

The in-window menu bar follows the RadioBOSS style:

  • File: Add folder to library… · Open playlist… · Save playlist… · Export log (CSV)… · Quit
  • Edit: Search the library · Clear the playlist
  • View: the seven tabs · Language ▸
  • Playback: Play · Pause · Stop · Next · Crossfade… · Silence detector… · Normalisation… · Sound cards…
  • Automation: Automation on/off · Preview… · Clocks · Grid · Categories · Advertising · Station settings
  • Broadcast: Streaming settings… · Start streaming · Stop streaming
  • Help: Manual (F1) · Keyboard shortcuts · Web panel and server · About RIARS

The same actions are available from the tabs and cards described in this manual. See Playback and Automation overview. The browser panel has a different, smaller layout: see Web panel.
