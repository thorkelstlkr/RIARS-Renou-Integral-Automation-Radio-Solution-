Live broadcasting

The On air tab (Ctrl+8) brings together what a presenter needs: the microphone, the sound bank and the mode the playlist moves on in.

Microphone

  • The MIC button (or F9 from any tab) opens and closes it, with a short fade so no click is heard. While it is open the button turns red and ON AIR shows.
  • When it opens, the music goes down by itself (12 dB by default) and comes back when it closes. To lower it only while you speak, choose While speaking in the settings.
  • The meter shows the level even with the microphone closed, so you can check your voice before going on air. Keep the peaks in the green or yellow.
  • If the microphone's card fails or is unplugged, the microphone falls silent and the error shows in red. The broadcast goes on.

RIARS does not send the microphone to the pre-listen headphones: to hear yourself, use the mixing desk or your audio interface's direct monitoring.

If you hear an echo

Many audio interfaces (a Roland UA-4FX, for example) send the microphone input straight to their output, with no delay. If RIARS plays it on that card as well, you hear yourself twice: the second copy arrives a fraction of a second late and sounds like an echo. If the card output is your on-air signal, listeners hear it too.

Tick The sound card already plays the microphone (direct monitoring). RIARS then stops playing the microphone on the card, which already does so with no delay, and adds it only to the Internet stream. The music still ducks when the microphone opens and the meter still works. If your interface has no direct monitoring, leave the box unticked; otherwise the microphone would not reach the card at all.

Sound bank

A grid of 6 × 4 buttons with 8 pages, for jingles, effects, sweepers and music beds. You can keep several banks, one per show for example, and pick the active one at the top.

  • Assign a sound: drag a track from the library (or a file from the Folders tab) onto a button, or click an empty button. In the editor, clicking Choose a track… shows the library list, which filters as you type; Choose file… takes any audio file on disk, in the library or not.
  • Fire: click the button, or keys 1–0 and Q–P for the first twenty buttons of the visible page. Page Up / Page Down change page. Esc or Stop all stops every bank sound.
  • Each button shows the time it has left while it plays. Up to 8 can play at once, over the music, and with the playlist stopped too.
  • Right-click a button: Edit… (label, colour, gain, mode and whether it lowers the music), Pre-listen and Clear.
Button modeWhat it does
FireEvery press starts it from the top.
ToggleOne press starts it, the next stops it.
LoopIt repeats until you stop it. Handy for music beds.

Short sounds (under 30 s) are loaded into memory when their page opens, so they play at once. Everything played from the bank goes into the proof-of-play log.

Playlist mode

ModeWhat it does
AutomaticThe playlist moves on by itself and the automation fills it.
AssistedThe playlist moves on by itself, but the automation adds nothing and makes no cuts: what you prepared plays.
ManualWhen each item ends the playlist stops and waits for Play (F5) or Next (F8).

In any mode, right-click ▸ Stop after this item in the playlist stops the broadcast when that item ends. Handy to come in and talk. The item shows ⏹.

Talking over the intro

When a song with intro marks starts, the panel shows in green the seconds left before the voice comes in; the last three in yellow. You can introduce the song and stop talking just in time.
