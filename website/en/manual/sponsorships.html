Sponsorships

A sponsorship is a campaign that is linked to a programme or a time span instead of being spread through ordinary ad breaks. It plays a sponsor message at the Opening and/or the Closing of the programme: “This programme comes to you thanks to…”.

Setting one up

In the Advertising tab, open Campaigns and select or create a campaign. In the Sponsorship section choose:

FieldMeaning
NoneNo sponsorship
ClockSponsor every period of a clock; choose the Clock
Grid ruleSponsor the span of a grid rule; choose the Grid rule

Then tick Opening and/or Closing. The spots of the campaign are the messages that play; add them under Spots like any other campaign. A sponsorship does not need a daily spot target: Spots per day can be 0 when the campaign exists only to sponsor a programme.

How it airs

When the sponsored clock or rule starts, RIARS plays the opening spot before the first song. At the end of the period it plays the closing spot. If the same programme continues into the next hour, the sponsorship is not repeated, so the opening is heard once at the real start of the programme.

What it is not

A sponsorship is not an Ad break slot in a clock. Ad breaks are filled by the traffic system from all eligible campaigns; a sponsorship is fixed to its programme. You can use both in the same station: a sponsored morning show with its own clock, and ordinary breaks at :15 and :45.

Tip: keep the sponsor message short and distinct from the programme audio. The proof-of-play log records it with the campaign, so the delivery report counts it.

See Advertisers and campaigns and Delivery report.
