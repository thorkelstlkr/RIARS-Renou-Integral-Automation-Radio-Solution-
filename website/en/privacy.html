Privacy

Privacy policy

RIARS does not collect or send usage data. It has no telemetry, no accounts and no ads.

When it connects to other systems

Only when you ask it to:

  • Completing track metadata. When you look up a track, RIARS sends its title and artist to MusicBrainz and downloads the cover from the Cover Art Archive. If you turn on AcoustID with your own key, it also sends the track's audio fingerprint and duration to AcoustID. The audio itself is never sent.
  • Streaming. The audio and the song title go to the Icecast server you configure.

Each of those services has its own privacy policy.

Remote control

The API, the web panel and the MCP server listen only on your own machine unless you open them to the network with --bind. Every request needs a token that exists only on your machine.

This website

The website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

For any question, open an issue on GitHub.
